# kikitori v0.4.0

This release lets kikitori use another computer's graphics card. If your laptop is too slow for the bigger recognition models, a desktop PC at home (or anywhere on your Tailscale network) can do the recognition and translation, and the subtitles come back to your laptop. The design is in `docs/specs/remote-compute.md` and `docs/specs/compute-capacity.md` (in Chinese).

## New features

### Use another computer for recognition and translation

- **Three roles, chosen in the left sidebar under "Processing"**:
  - **Just this computer**: as before.
  - **Let another computer do the work**: your audio is sent to the compute host and the subtitles come back. Nothing has to be downloaded on this computer.
  - **Do the work for other computers**: this computer becomes a compute host.
- **A bar across the top of the window always shows the current role**, with its own color, icon and text: "This computer does the work", "Using a compute host: GTX 1080 Ti · 3 ms", or "Compute host mode: Waiting for another computer to connect".
- **Pairing**: press "Pair" on your computer; it shows six digits. Type them on the compute host, then confirm. After that the two computers recognize each other.
- **Secure by default**: the connection is encrypted (TLS 1.3) and only paired computers are accepted. The compute host listens only on your home or office network or Tailscale, never on every network.
- **Short network drops are survived**: after a drop of up to about 30 seconds the session reconnects by itself with no missing or repeated lines. On longer drops you are offered to switch back to this computer.
- **Fast**: in our tests, using a compute host on the same network added about 6 ms per line, and about 38 ms over Tailscale.

### See what the compute host can run, and change its models from your computer

- Before you start, the sidebar shows the compute host's graphics card (for example "GTX 1080 Ti · 11 GB") and each recognition model's fit: **Recommended**, **Runs fine**, **May be tight** or **Too heavy**. The estimate includes a translation model running on the same card.
- **Change the compute host's models from your computer**: in "Let another computer do the work" mode, the Models page shows "Models on the compute host". Pick the live and final recognition models and the translation model; the compute host saves them and uses them from the next session. You can also download a missing model on the compute host and watch its progress.
- **"Too heavy" is a warning, not a block**: choosing it asks once more and explains that subtitles may become slow, fall behind or not start. The warning stays visible until you choose a combination that fits.
- The compute host's own screen shows who changed what.

### Compute load chart

- While subtitles are running, the left sidebar shows **Computing load**: a colored bar, a 2-minute line chart and a plain-language status ("Plenty of headroom", "Close to the limit", "Can't keep up: try a smaller model"), with graphics memory use and recognition speed underneath. It works on this computer and, in "Let another computer do the work" mode, shows the compute host's load.

### Compute host mode

- The Transcript page shows a **Compute host** panel: who is using this computer, how many lines it has processed, this computer's load, the models in use, and buttons to pair a new computer or stop.
- While another computer is connected, Start is disabled with an explanation, because the graphics card is in use. The other computer's subtitles are never shown on the compute host.
- **A compute host without a screen**: run `kikitori` in a terminal and choose "Compute host". From there you can start serving, pair, choose and download models, and see the current load.

## Upgrading

- Your settings carry over; the new option starts as "Just this computer".
- To use a compute host, install kikitori 0.4.0 on both computers.
